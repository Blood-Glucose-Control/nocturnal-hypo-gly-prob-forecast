\section{Episodic RMSE by Forecast Horizon}\label{app:episodicrmse-tables}

Episodic RMSE at horizon $k$ (steps of 5\,min) is:

$$\mathrm{RMSE}_i(k) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{k}\sum_{t=1}^{k}(y_{i,t}-\hat{y}_{i,t})^2}$$

Each table cell represents:

$$\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathrm{RMSE}_i(k)\pm\,\mathrm{std}$$

across $k$ time steps using the best-performing configuration.
