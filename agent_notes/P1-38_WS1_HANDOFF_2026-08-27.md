## Why some modules became longer during WS1

This wave prioritized behavior-safe extraction and explicit contracts over immediate LOC reduction:

- Long methods were split into named helpers, but those helpers initially live in the same module for reviewability and parity checks.
- Additional guardrails/logging and explicit override controls were added to prevent silent behavior drift.
- Small compatibility shims were temporarily retained while call sites migrated.

Net: complexity is reduced (better boundaries/tests), even where raw LOC is flat or slightly higher. LOC reduction is expected in the follow-up cleanup/removal pass once call-site and parity evidence is complete.
